# Current-checkpoint wipe statistics

The common ActivityAttemptStats accumulator is owned by one session and generation. It starts empty at each new checkpoint; another attempt at the same checkpoint increments its attempt number. Normal checkpoint progression resets counts and starts attempt1. It never reads persistent Minecraft statistics. Rows show actual final blows, positive applied health+shield loss, its boss subset, and accepted activity down-life deaths. Damage is published after Combat.damage has computed actual before/after pools and root recipient; overkill, immune/missed hits and neutral actor removal do not mint values. Captured AmmoGeneration is carried without rereading the equipped gun. Devils adapter requires its own actor runtime, same world, current actor generation, retained member and exact current connected ServerPlayer. Raw Husk fixtures in other tests do not pass this adapter.

At committed wipe, the immutable snapshot freezes once. Late damage/down events, disconnects or personal departure cannot rewrite the shown table. A player leaving before freeze keeps the current-attempt row marked LEFT; a retained disconnected player is marked DISCONNECTED. The next checkpoint/attempt excludes permanently departed members and starts all counters at zero. Subscription close removes the exact observer; an observer RuntimeException detaches only that observer so damage/rewards continue. The common dispatcher owns no activity statistics.
