### AI Mode reply for Destiny 2 flinch direction toward reticle away source damage

In Destiny 2, the behavior of incoming flinch functions in two distinct ways depending entirely on whether your crosshair is currently **on-target or off-target** at the moment you receive damage:[](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZgHrOzAVl9UG_1TnmVv4BW40DNJqmS2rj1ie06JFfQWJACRpDdXz3NsWSfH9Vk9Zag9f6CaeeCx_vEAGWGVjnuhyWksrzR0s3tVbpUs2fevhupZ8lEb30QpAQj0WOtswNd_qqLborA)

1. Off-Target: Flinch Pulls TOWARD the Reticle/Crit Spot

If your crosshair is hovering outside the enemy's critical hitbox when you take damage, **flinch acts as a form of aim assist, dynamically pulling your reticle toward the target's head**.[](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESnQEB6zswFQkvJw__M-5vovlPT2sumq3si6ihDfY-s6XxeZzbDIC1HsE-2NLaBgOgZf3x_5uMxj7IeF07ueN6IMZZZvsr9l3FgU_m40p0I9vIf9KotuN59quZjV4u1p2ToJLQQ2ONwKSJlnetWoyGlXL6ZG2WYDVD348TlH2yEPrVHzt2AL62IIhDi8XsUx1KxZ1wcGchtfCCCRrMm6Ec)

*   **The "Clock" Effect:** No matter what direction you are off-target (above, below, left, or right), the game's mechanics cause incoming fire to jump your crosshair closer to the enemy's head.

*   **The Follow-Up Shot:** Getting flinched _once_ while off-target pulls you closer to the head, often allowing you to land a shot you otherwise would have missed.[](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESkwEB6zswFXn5ZBGiPdxLAYJT_zN1rlmchruueMLf1Z9Y_vq4BvOEt99pPA9wWvb9LMxyBkfoZAT4GIg_tsmeX1_UEpeam01Nc7eD2FkL-ESToN5z4FwSUdSaw8VwSZ0e_2WMMvOmzOmN7JHMFiDz6BxtHdfNqtBo6mThAFk1kawdwR572i41B-z_nuhiLDugA_v37BI)

2. On-Target: Flinch Kicks AWAY Based on Source Damage

If you are already cleanly aiming at the enemy's head when you are shot, **flinch aggressively kicks your reticle away from the target**. The direction of this exit kick is dictated entirely by where the incoming damage originates:[](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZgHrOzAVh9jR42SD-FmL0BPWvmH1ZyGqq4ut2rKlLXk3nA2Xoz5FjouVtFcvYEEQBCrSXifJG_5wPcRuFRrr51Ppq8-HuGyE8_1tDmNMrdBbekRnEf6ZNluhfqCDN5rJlg2YUZH5Ew)

*   **Head-on / Frontal Damage:** Causes a heavy **vertical** flinch, forcing your scope directly upward.

*   **Flank / Side Damage:** Causes a **horizontal** flinch, kicking your crosshair sideways in the _opposite_ direction of where you are getting shot from.

*   **Subsequent Shots:** Getting hit a _second_ time consecutively while your reticle is displaced by the first flinch will often drag it back toward the target center due to the off-target tracking mechanic.[](https://www.google.com/goto?url=CAESZgHrOzAVh9jR42SD-FmL0BPWvmH1ZyGqq4ut2rKlLXk3nA2Xoz5FjouVtFcvYEEQBCrSXifJG_5wPcRuFRrr51Ppq8-HuGyE8_1tDmNMrdBbekRnEf6ZNluhfqCDN5rJlg2YUZH5Ew)
