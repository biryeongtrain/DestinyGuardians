# Brigand's Law revision 2

The first draft was rejected: the thin upper bow became a broad cream roof, the cream/navy receiver collapsed into a navy slab, and the source solid tapered grip became a narrow stepped strap. V2 was redrawn from the actual static .blend and its official default icon.

The new 123-block design has an exposed satin steel barrel and deep flared muzzle, two thin ivory top bows with a real open gap, a thick compact cream receiver, gold side linkage, narrow swept guard, joined steel tines, and a filled tapered grip with a source-inspired blue spider seal. Forward geometry is compressed 14 percent around the grip-root anchor. Grip sections lean back more strongly while the mid-palm region stays close to the inherited sidearm contract.

Warm ivory relief and navy paint patches are filtered from the source. Metal, gold and coated ivory use distinct finish responses. The supplied .blend has no armature and no animation actions; Dead Man Walking's 61-pose presentation is reused, not presented as original-source reload animation. Native hand contact and reload acceptance remain pending.

Original, rejected V1 and current V2 are preserved in review/original-v1-v2.png; V1 geometry and atlas snapshots are in review/v1-*.
