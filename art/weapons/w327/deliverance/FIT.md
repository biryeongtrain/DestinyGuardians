# Deliverance presentation fit

The supplied .blend was opened with auto-execution disabled and its evaluated meshes and static bones inspected. All supplied actions lists are empty; these files provide weapon geometry, not animated hand/wrist/elbow contact. The role palette follows the source and the official default icon. The authored model is a block reinterpretation, not a mesh-equivalence claim.

The hollow_words presentation supplies the grip/ADS transforms and reload poses. Its recorded pose bank is in manifest.json. Moving geometry is separated by the runtime WeaponSkinAssets role contract; Heritage remains shell-loaded, Forbearance moves its front breach barrel, and rocket models use a separate payload. Native contact, moving-part clearance and return must be checked by the master.

Offline ADS compares the actual WeaponAimPresentation leaf (including resolved parents), not an obsolete reload body. The center x 6.6..9.4 rear-two-thirds check is conservative and does not prove reticle alignment. Base overlay reticles are retained. See adscheck.json for exact leaf path and result.

Five final views: west, east, top, q, front. source-comparison.png pairs evaluated-source and authored geometry. Parse, positive box bounds, unique names, UV bounds, atlas budget, source hash preservation and exposed coplanar checks are in validation.json. In-game rendering, FPS and 10-20 display checks have not been performed by this worker.
