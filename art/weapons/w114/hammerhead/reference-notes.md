# Hammerhead W114 reference review

Reviewed the 16 selected frames in gameplay-contact.png from Aztecross, 2024-04-25, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1lHv7453rUI at 101.50–106.40 seconds. This is the empty manual reload: visible receiver/cover and feed handling precede the HUD changing from 0 to 68. The surrounding Rewind Rounds narration is not used as evidence for the mechanism or timing. The scorecards, combat perk refills, ability gestures, and weapon swaps in preliminary probes are excluded.

The weapon rolls sideways, the left hand reaches the receiver, the long top cover lifts nearly upright, and the receiver/feed area becomes exposed. The left hand presents the large feed box under/alongside the receiver, handles the belt/receiver, brings the cover down, and performs a final gesture before ready. Some feed handling is hand-occluded. There is no claim of a measured extraction trajectory or an exact box latch boundary. The original FBX additionally identifies Magazine, Cover and linked Bullet skin clusters: this is stronger evidence than a silhouette or bounding box.

The three authored parts are original whole cuboids: fixed body (including the small latch/charging handle), Magazine + its linked Bullet1..8 feed belt, and Cover (including its original sight). The original Cover TransformLink supplies the hinge; no invented cover, texture, UV, closure face or replacement geometry is added. Connected components, material and rigid skin semantics, clipped source surface area, and seven-point point-to-triangle distances are recorded in the audits. Mixed seam cubes necessarily approximate the high-resolution source boundary.

The product is an authored reference-informed approximation with 21 normalized samples. All numeric transforms, interpolation, framing and local feed path are authored. The feed assembly is rigid, its belt does not deform, and hands/IK and independent latch/charging-handle motion are absent. This is not extracted original animation. Original catalog reload duration, audio, ammo and perks remain under shared master code; the video's speed may be modified and is not copied. Native left/right hand, carrier and visibility QA remains pending master.
