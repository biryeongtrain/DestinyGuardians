# Cold Denial W138 offline review

Actual reference: `/tmp/destiny-weapons-w31/references/cold_denial.jpg` plus inert Cold Denial FBX and CDFrame/CDMag/CDSight diffuse PNGs. The reference was opened using local view_image. The undyed raw source has 24,191 triangles across five included meshes; 1,992 Tube/MagGlass triangles have no separate diffuse map and are shown neutral in the comparison. The excluded reticle card is not reconstructed as an alpha plane.

The final full-only candidate has 100 authored opaque cuboids and 600 explicit faces. Long upper/lower receiver and stock cores, raked pistol grip and opaque bright magazine are filled volumes. Two eight-wall muzzles have real recessed throats; the reflex sight and trigger guard have actual openings. Bronze conduit bundles stand proud and change direction in three structural segments, with five strands painted in each face UV. Silver action-capsule trim has real depth around a filled receiver. No mesh island, polygon, voxel conversion or alpha side shells are used.

Identity: long squared bullpup silhouette, teal flowing surface pattern, repeated eye emblems, bronze stepped conduits, dark medallion/perforation strips, open reflex sight, silver action surround and pale rear magazine. Reference-guided decorative circles are shallow UV recesses, not through-holes. All six faces of every cube have independent proportional atlas rectangles and two-pixel extruded gutters in an opaque 1024 atlas. Teal marbling, eye motifs, small rail teeth, recess rings and material scoring are authored paint without stochastic noise. Ace87/Izanagi79 informed continuous volumes and face detail; cube count was not a substitute for silhouette quality.

Verified source mapping: `anchor + scale * [raw.X, raw.Z, raw.Y]`, all six W30 bounds within 2e-6. Each of five mesh models is a root object; local rotation approximately [-90,0,0], scale100 and negligible translation were inspected. Armature transforms are not applied to the resting mesh. Native -Z muzzle, scale 32.75106539638079, anchor/grip [8,8,8] and every canonical display transform are retained. Canonical and gameplay/reload/audio sources are read-only. Full-only art does not introduce a magazine split or replacement pivot.

Comparison: actual appearance photograph, then matched-camera side/front/rear oblique rows for raw source, canonical 241-cube model and candidate; detail views show bore depth, sight, trigger and magazine. inspection.bbmodel embeds the exact atlas and semantic groups, for later user/master opening only.

Limitations: faceted curves and simplified small bevels; authored paint approximates source material patterns rather than recovering PBR; magazine is opaque static color rather than emission/refraction. Offline CPU renders do not establish native pack loading, 26.3 rotation support, first/third-person holding, ADS clearance, reload trajectory or 10-20 display behavior. Those checks remain pending master. No Gradle, game, GUI, Blender, Blockbench or other workers were used.
