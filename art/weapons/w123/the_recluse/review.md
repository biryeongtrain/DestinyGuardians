# W126 The Recluse semantic redesign

66 solid cuboids: body 62, magazine 4; 396 opaque faces, 396 independent UV islands on 1024² (46.5% content occupancy before padding). No source cap/wall extrusion or alpha receiver skins.

The receiver, optic hood, short stock, lower diagonal frame, grips and power rail are deliberately sized functional solids. Eight sight bars and eight muzzle crown staves keep real apertures; the trigger guard is three structural bars. The white optic hood has a reference-backed red four-cell charge window; inherited bird paint is restricted to the rear fields. Small source perforations, seams and fasteners use selective FBX source-UV relief; clean default paint and burgundy bird masks are repacked into each new face frame. Surface detail does not create additional cubes.

Raw FBX triangles vs new side and oblique views are rendered with matching offline cameras in comparison.png. The raw column uses W31 color lookup and is not a source-shader restoration. The full model display, source frame, scale and anchor remain unchanged; separate magazine vertices stay in the existing frame. Past W124/W125 QA is retained.

Live pack substitution, first/third person, ADS, detached magazine reload and 10–20 simultaneous displays belong to master. No native success or performance result is claimed here.
