# Whisper of the Worm — W350 revision 3

User requested higher detail, then supplied an authoritative render. R3 geometry and finish are generated by `scripts/art/w350/power_whisper_r3.py`, using the accepted W327 native authored-closed-loft adapter. Read `r3/HANDOFF.md` for review scope, exact references, counts and limitations. Do not run the older generic R2 generator for Whisper when retaining R3.

The actual source .blend was reopened read-only in an owned headless Blender process. Transparent FX-card materials were excluded only from additional geometry-evidence renders. Native geometry is authored; no ripped mesh export/decimation/voxelization is used. Original source/archive hashes and all original proof remain unchanged. R2 proof is preserved in r2/ and /tmp/exotic-expansion-20261109/whisper-r3/before-r2/.

R3 uses skull brow/cheek/mandible masses and recessed eye/mouth, closed swept bone roots and teeth, a chamfered long shroud, separated diagonal receiver and lower housing with exposed cylindrical bolt, two optic feet/collars/turret with blue inset lenses, and a continuous sloped grip/open stock with inset plates. Latest user-render color takes priority over gray/gold source Workbench materials: white luminous front bone, black mechanical body, sparse cyan vents/spots and one short receiver stripe. White body veins were removed.

995 actual emitted native elements (body980 + magazine15), atlas2048x1024. Skull338 elements use native light_emission15/shadefalse, optical20 use8/shadefalse, remaining637 including orbital floors are nonemissive. No full-side glow billboard or bloom effect is used. The offline native JSON renderer honors alpha and approximates per-element light emission without scene cast light/bloom.

All item routes, 61 reload poses, 21 aim samples, contacts and moving pivots remain R2 contracts. Original weapon source has no actions or player hand animation. Native pack/client, grip/ADS/reload, FPS and final aesthetic acceptance remain pending master QA.

Target codec caveat: `shade:false` is retained as a legacy authored flag; its effect on26.3 was not verified and may be ignored. Only `light_emission` integer0..15 support was verified. Native Vision will assess whether the white cluster retains enough facet detail.
