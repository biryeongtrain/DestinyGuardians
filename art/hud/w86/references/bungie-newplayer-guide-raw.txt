## What is a Rewards Pass?

Rewards Passes give players access to new content within a _Destiny 2_ Season. Throughout a Season, players will gain access to a new story, tackle new challenges, complete new activities, and unlock new gear.

In each [Year](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/4408041224852) of _Destiny 2_, some seasonal content will persist beyond its Season if players own that Season’s Rewards Pass. Players who don’t own that Season’s Rewards Pass and would like to access this content must purchase or upgrade to the latest[_Destiny 2_ Digital Deluxe Edition](https://www.bungie.net/destiny/buy/). This content will be available until the current [Year](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/4408041224852) ends.

![Image 2](https://help.bungie.net/hc/article_attachments/29834791094420)

  

Included in a Rewards Pass are Premium Track rewards for Seasonal Ranks which give players access to rewards earlier than the Free Track, with some rewards being exclusive to the Premium Track. More information about Rewards Pass Ranks can be found below.

A player's Rewards Pass and its progression will be able to travel with a player via [Cross Save](https://www.bungie.net/CrossSave). Players should enable Cross Save before applying the Rewards Pass to their account if they plan to play on multiple platforms or have multiple platform accounts.

* * *

## Purchasing or Acquiring a Rewards Pass

Each Rewards Pass costs 1000 Silver and can be purchased during a specific Season. A Rewards Pass for a specific season can’t be purchased after that Season ends unless players purchase or upgrade to the latest [_Destiny 2_ Digital Deluxe Edition](https://www.bungie.net/destiny/buy).

**PLEASE NOTE!**

*   Logging into _Destiny 2_ on any platform will **instantly** unlock Rewards Passes acquired from a _Destiny 2_ expansion bundle, even if no characters have been created on that platform or account yet.

*   If players want to use [Cross Save](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049498031), it should be fully activated **before** logging into _Destiny 2_ on a platform where Rewards Passes were purchased from an expansion bundle, otherwise, those Rewards Passes may become lost.

## Seasonal Ranks

Seasonal Ranks are available to all players and allow them to unlock rewards by playing _Destiny 2_ and earning XP. Each rank requires a certain amount of XP to unlock.

![Image 3: season_pass_EN.jpg](https://help.bungie.net/hc/article_attachments/29834769038356)

  

There are two tracks of a Seasonal Rank: Free and Premium. The Free track is available to all players regardless of what _Destiny 2_ content they own, whereas the Premium track is only available to players who own the current Rewards Pass.The Premium Track gives players access to rewards earlier than the Free Track, with some rewards being exclusive to the Premium Track.

Once a season ends, the ranks will reset, and players will be able to earn new rewards from the tracks for the next Season. Seasonal Ranks for previous Seasons are not available to players who purchase or access previous Seasons during the current [Year](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/4408041224852).

## Artifacts and Mods

Artifacts are available to all players and allow new ways to customize Guardian builds every Season by unlocking Seasons mods to insert in armor. Earning XP will level up the Seasonal Artifact, letting players unlock the mods in a tier until more tiers are unlocked.

Seasonal armor mods are used to elevate reload speeds, ability regen, and more, to higher levels. Starting in Season 12, armor will have a mod slot that accepts mods introduced into the game throughout a full [Year](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/4408041224852).

![Image 4: undying_artifact_EN.jpg](https://help.bungie.net/hc/article_attachments/29834791095572)

  

While leveling up and unlocking mods, players will also increase the Power of the Artifact, which will continue increasing until the end of the Season. Artifact Power adds directly to a player's overall Power and is unlocked account-wide.

Seasonal Artifacts and Seasonal mods will go away at the end of the Season they were introduced in, but mods will eventually be added back into the game.

## Vital Information and Troubleshooting

**INSTANT UNLOCK**

Logging into _Destiny 2_ on any platform will **instantly** unlock Rewards Passes acquired from a _Destiny 2_ expansion bundle, even if no characters have been created on that platform or account yet.

[Cross Save](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049498031)should be fully activated **before** logging into _Destiny 2_ on a platform where Rewards Passes were purchased from an expansion bundle, otherwise, those Rewards Passes may become lost.

**GAME SHARING**

Rewards Pass Premium Track unlocks and rewards **cannot** be game shared, and every player must purchase a Rewards Pass on their account to access that Season's seasonal content.

Game sharing is a platform feature and is [not officially supported by Bungie](https://help.bungie.net/articles/360048719412).

**TWO SEASONS TO CLAIM EARNED REWARDS**

Players have two Seasons to claim earned Premium Track ranks rewards from a Season Pass or Rewards Pass, including the Season it was active in where XP unlocked the rank. After a Season ends, players can claim the earned rewards found in that Rewards Pass on their [Previous and Current Rewards Pass page.](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/Seasons/PreviousSeason) Players may need to click their platform ID and then click the platform ID again for it to load the page correctly. This page can also be found on the Destiny Companion App.

Players need to have earned the corresponding Rewards Pass rank when the Season was active to collect previous Premium Track Rewards Pass rewards. Any earned Rewards Pass rewards older than two Seasons are unavailable to collect. Bungie does not grant previous uncollected Rewards Pass items. Players should ensure that they collect all available rewards prior to the Rewards Pass becoming unavailable to access.

Additionally, Bungie does not offer refunds or grants for lost Bright Engrams earned from the Rewards Pass.

**REWARDS PASSES FROM OLD BUNDLE PURCHASES**

Season Passes and Rewards Passes will not unlock for players who purchase a bundle that doesn't contain the newest expansion. The current _[Year of Destiny 2](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/4408041224852)_ is what the newest expansion includes.

**MISSING REWARDS PASSES**

If the above scenarios don't apply to why a player may be missing their Rewards Passes, they can [contact us](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049497351) to start an investigation.
